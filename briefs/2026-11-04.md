## 2. Breaches and confirmed incidents

### GitBook (developer documentation SaaS): "AI agent-driven" intrusion [NEW]
- Attackers obtained some infrastructure credentials at about 18:00 UTC on 2 Nov. GitBook confirmed the incident on 3 Nov, suspended the app and authenticated docs, and restored service later that day.
- Possibly exposed: API tokens, integration credentials, Git Sync secrets and questions submitted to GitBook Assistant. No evidence customer content was changed.
- GitBook revoked all personal API tokens; customers using its MCP server must re-authenticate. No customer count. A post-incident report is promised.
- PancakeSwap took its docs offline and moved them to its own infrastructure.
- Single secondary source; no GitBook primary statement found. https://financefeeds.com/gitbook-hit-by-ai-agent-driven-cyberattack-disrupting-documentation-for-crypto-projects/
- **Action:** if your teams publish docs or sync repositories through GitBook, rotate Git Sync and integration secrets.

### Chainstack (blockchain node SaaS): production breach [NEW]
- Entered a development environment through a "third-party component" on 26 Oct and escalated into production; detected and cut off 31 Oct; disclosed 3 Nov.
- Exposed: organisation and account names, member emails, password hashes. Node credentials treated as exposed. Customers told to rotate node credentials, regenerate API keys and change passwords.
- THN notes overlaps with the ARTEX AI campaign (also behind WorkOS); not confirmed by Chainstack.
- Sources:
  - https://chainstack.com/security-incident-nov-2026/
  - https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/chainstack-reports-production-systems.html

## 5. AI attacks and trends

- **GTIG / Mandiant: "The Rise of LLMJacking" [OLDER, 2 Nov]:**
  - Fraudulent AI-model charges of more than $100,000 in a day at some victims and up to $1M cumulative. Victims in finance, retail, technology and transport across North America, South America and APAC.

## 12. Deep dive: Gorbag, Russia-aligned espionage against Ukraine's justice system (ESET, 3 Nov)

**Why it matters:** firms advising Ukrainian government, justice or reconstruction programmes, or donor and military-aid logistics, sit next to this group's targets. It phishes from real, compromised government mailboxes, exploits an archive-tool flaw patched in 2025, and steals the VPN, RDP, password-vault and signing-key files that let other actors in.

### Sources
- https://www.welivesecurity.com/en/eset-research/gorbag-orcs-border-harvesting-intelligence-ukraine/
- IOCs: https://github.com/eset/malware-ioc/tree/master/gorbag
- https://therecord.media/russia-linked-cyber-spies-target-ukraine-war-support
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/russia-aligned-gorbag-hackers-exploit.html
- https://www.virusbulletin.com/conference/vb2026/abstracts/gorbag-orcs-border/

### Summary
- Newly named Russia-aligned cyber-espionage group, active since at least February 2025, targeting only Ukraine.
- Victims: about a quarter justice and law-enforcement bodies; about a quarter local administrations, mostly front-line city councils that were nearly all also hit by air strikes; the rest defence and logistics, including a hub for foreign military aid. Infosecurity reports BeautyStealer took electronic signature keys from a Ukrainian certification centre.
- ESET's hypotheses for the aims: monitoring war-crimes investigations, identifying collaborators, assessing battle damage and tracking military supply.

### Attribution and confidence
- ESET calls it "Russia-aligned" without a formal confidence level. THN reports overlaps with CERT-UA's UAC-0226 (GIFTEDCROOK) and Trend Micro's SHADOW-EARTH-066.
- ESET suggests, as one possible explanation, that Gorbag is an initial access broker for other groups. Unconfirmed.

### Timeline
- Feb 2025: first known activity (Prime ReverseShell). 28 Mar 2025: C2 first seen. Late Mar 2025: first attack on a justice entity.
- Through 2025: macro-enabled Excel lures. Early 2026: RAR archives exploiting WinRAR CVE-2025-8088.
- 2 Jun 2026: BeautyStealer exfiltration server first seen. 3 Nov 2026: ESET publishes (also presented at Virus Bulletin, 14 Oct).

### Initial access
- 2025: spearphishing from compromised Ukrainian local-government mailboxes, with lures about fines and taxes. The `.xlsm` macro decodes base64 stored in cells, drops a payload to `%ProgramData%` and runs it.
- 2026: a RAR archive exploits CVE-2025-8088 (path traversal via alternate data streams), shows a decoy PDF, drops a LNK into the Startup folder and places an encrypted payload and PowerShell loader in `%ProgramData%`.

### Tooling
- **Prime ReverseShell:** PowerShell reverse shell wrapped as .NET with PS2EXE, derived from a public repository; HTTPS C2. One variant carries a comment addressed to AI tools telling them not to analyse the file.
- **BeautyStealer** (C++, v0.9–2.0): browser cookies, passwords, local state and history (Chrome, Edge, Opera, Firefox) and OpenVPN configs; files from Desktop, Documents, Downloads, removable and mapped drives with extensions including .kdbx, .ovpn, .rdp, .pfx, .jks and .zs2 (Ukrainian qualified-signature keys), plus Office, PDF and archives. XOR strings from v1.2, RC4 in v2.0. Deletes itself after exfiltration.
- Persistence: LNK in the Startup folder.

### C2 and exfiltration
- Early versions send archives to a Telegram chat via `sendDocument`; from v1.2 to a custom FastAPI server.

### Hunting (built from ESET's artefacts)
1. Find WinRAR installs older than the CVE-2025-8088 fix; hunt RAR extraction writing `.lnk` files into `\Start Menu\Programs\Startup\` or alternate-data-stream paths.
2. Excel with macros writing an executable or script to `%ProgramData%`, then a child process from that path.
3. PS2EXE-compiled binaries named "Windows Telemetry" making outbound HTTPS.
4. Non-browser processes calling `api.telegram.org/bot*/sendDocument`; large archive uploads from workstations.
5. Processes reading browser Login Data, Cookies, Local State, `.kdbx`, `.ovpn` and `.pfx` files and then deleting themselves.
6. Block and retro-hunt the two IPs.
