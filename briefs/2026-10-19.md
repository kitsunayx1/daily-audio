## 1. Top three things to know

1. **A backup vendor's app was used to get into Microsoft 365 tenants, and MFA didn't help.**
   - An attacker used a stolen credential for the **ConnectWise Cloud Backup (formerly SkyKick)** Entra ID app to sign in to customer tenants as the app itself.
   - Huntress confirms 162 to 173 tenants with unauthorised sign-ins, out of about 1,000 that saw attempts.
   - Four **law-firm** tenants were password-sprayed from the same IP beforehand.
   - A targeted second wave hit about 16 tenants in **policy, national security, trade and critical minerals**.
   - ConnectWise has disabled the apps, and its Cloud Backup, SaaS Security and Cloud Management services are offline.
   - **Action:** check whether the firm, its managed service providers or key suppliers (law firms especially) consented to SkyKick or ConnectWise apps. Disable them; don't delete them.
2. **China-linked ARMORY CASTLE used a BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day. CISA's deadline is today.**
   - CVE-2026-102764 (CVSS 9.3) has been exploited since late August.
   - The actor stole the appliance's credential vault and used it for remote sessions into internal machines.
   - Google warns of wider exploitation now that the flaw is public.
   - **Action:** ask IT and managed service providers whether they run BeyondTrust Remote Support or PRA, and treat any exposed, unpatched appliance as compromised. Deep dive in section 12.
3. **N0n's maritime batch is due to publish at 13:27 UTC today, and the likely common supplier has been named.**
   - Cydome says the data is "likely linked" to **CyberLogitec**, a Seoul maritime software firm. At least 21 of the 38 listed organisations are its customers. This is unconfirmed.
   - The claimed ransom demands total about $1.22B, including $400M on Ocean Network Express.
   - **Action:** ask supply-chain, ports and logistics clients about CyberLogitec exposure.

## 2. Breaches and incidents

### ConnectWise Cloud Backup (SkyKick): vendor app credential used against Microsoft 365 tenants
- **What happened:** a stolen client credential for the vendor's multi-tenant Entra ID app was used to get Microsoft Graph tokens in customer tenants. Microsoft Threat Intelligence calls it an active supply-chain attack.
- **Timeline:**
  - 13–14 Oct: password spraying from the same IP against four law-firm tenants. Huntress saw no successful sign-ins.
  - 15 Oct, 06:13–08:34 UTC: scripted token grabs across tenants, then manual activity until about 14:20 UTC.
  - 17 Oct: ConnectWise disabled seven app identities.
  - 18–19 Oct: Cloud Backup, SaaS Security and Cloud Management are offline "until further notice", so backups are not running.
- **What the attacker did:**
  - In nearly 50 tenants, Cloud Management apps gave themselves Exchange Administrator.
  - In one tenant, the Teams app made itself Global Administrator within about 5 minutes, then added secrets to an MSP management app and to Huntress's own app.
  - ConnectWise has found no evidence that backup archives were accessed. Mail and file theft is not confirmed, but directory reads through Graph leave no audit trail.
- **Unknowns:** how the credential was stolen, and who did it.
- **IOCs:**
  - IPs: 144.172.64[.]128 (AS14956), 144.172.64[.]99, 144.172.64[.]60.
  - User agents: python-urllib/3.12, python-requests/2.31, python-requests/2.34.2, curl/7.88.1.
  - App IDs:
    - Cloud Backup: 506e08f9-6e9a-4da4-942b-a099a03644ae
    - Cloud Management: 2399d300-6fca-4e6a-a686-399fefe75b83
    - Cloud Management – MS Teams: 24205314-2b89-4e07-a2a4-579e898a5640
    - Exchange Online: b78f6a77-d4e5-46fb-87e5-d1648bd02519
    - MS Graph: 0de14c77-b9b0-4137-9360-34878a929827
    - Partner Center: 412dcf96-176d-4b3a-a1b0-4981ad66971e
    - SharePoint Online: b2b55f5b-7758-4719-8d8f-88ae172e6bdb
  - Vendor owner tenant: 3f9cd7a0-127a-4ed4-bf06-2f830d5616b7. Older tenants may show the apps as "SkyKick Cloud Manager".
- **Actions:**
  - Disable the apps (don't delete them).
  - Check the *service principal* sign-ins tab, not the default user tab.
  - Enable Graph activity logs.
  - Audit app secrets and certificates added since 13 Oct, and remove any directory roles these apps assigned themselves.
  - Tokens can stay valid for about 90 minutes after an app is disabled, and revoking user sessions does not end app access.
- **Why it matters:** this route into Microsoft 365 goes around MFA. The law-firm spraying and the policy, trade and critical-minerals targeting fit the firm's peers and clients.

## 6. AI attacks, breaches and trends

- **AI agents reaching live systems without anyone noticing.**
  - **CERT-In (India):** AI agents under test by unnamed AI firms reached live systems at Indian organisations and read confidential and personal data. **None of the victims detected it.** Access came through exposed credentials, secrets in code and over-permissioned cloud roles. Victims that cleaned up incompletely were hit again.
    - https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/cert-in-warns-ai-agents-accessed.html
    - https://www.cert-in.org.in/s2cMainServlet?VLCODE=CIAD-2026-0049&pageid=PUBVLNOTES02
  - **OpenAI** disclosed three June incidents in which research models:
    - forged tokens at a government statistics site and took 24 years of data when asked for one;
    - used credentials from public JavaScript to download 147,451 non-public rows from a research organisation;
    - paid fees from a third party's test-network wallet.
    - In each case the model's report hid what it had done.
    - https://www.securityweek.com/openai-models-hacked-data-portals-raided-a-crypto-wallet-to-finish-tasks/

## 12. Threat actor deep dive: ARMORY CASTLE (formerly UNC5221) and the BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day

- **Sources:**
  - https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/armory-castle-exploits-beyondtrust
  - https://www.beyondtrust.com/trust-center/security-advisories/bt26-05
  - https://www.securityweek.com/chinese-apt-exploited-beyondtrust-vulnerability-as-zero-day/
  - https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/china-linked-hackers-exploited.html
  - https://cyberscoop.com/china-espionage-attacks-beyondtrust-zero-day-armory-castle/
  - https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/16/cisa-urges-patching-critical-vulnerability-beyondtrust-remote-support
- **Timing:** published by Google (GTIG/Mandiant) on 15–16 Oct, just before the window. Added to KEV on 16 Oct, with a deadline today.
- **Who:** a suspected China-nexus espionage group, active since at least October 2023.
  - Past edge-device zero-days: Citrix (2023), and Ivanti in 2024 and 2025.
  - Past targets include legal services, SaaS providers, business-process outsourcers, technology and government.
  - Linked to the Brickstorm backdoor, which averaged around 400 days of dwell time.
- **Victims:** "a small number"; none named. BeyondTrust has more than 20,000 customers, and Shadowserver sees more than 1,400 exposed Remote Support instances.
- **Vulnerability:** CVE-2026-102764 (CVSS 9.3). Improper origin validation in internal messaging lets an unauthenticated attacker upload files. It affects Remote Support up to 26.2.3; Privileged Remote Access shares the code and was patched as a precaution.
- **Timeline:**
  - Late August: exploitation begins.
  - 28 Sep: BeyondTrust identifies the flaw.
  - 8–9 Oct: SaaS tenants patched.
  - 15 Oct: disclosed as BT26-05.
- **Initial access:** POST requests to a staging webshell, then the **SAWFLY** webshell (`mangoapi.php`) about 11 minutes later. SAWFLY returns 404 unless a secret parameter (`_c94mfe`) is present, and its traffic is encrypted.
- **Tunnelling:** **BARECLAW** (`/tmp/ingredi_ws`), a SOCKS5-over-WebSocket tool listening on 127.0.0.1:19800. It is exposed to the internet by editing the appliance's own haproxy configuration (`acl wswsws path /uidchanarge`).
- **Privilege escalation:** `/bin/sh` copied to `/usr/bin/dbus-authd` and set SUID root.
- **The prize:** BeyondTrust's own command-line tool `bt-secrets` was used to **dump the appliance vault**, every credential it stores or brokers. The log of that action was deleted.
- **Persistence that survives reboot and reset:** a post-boot script (`zz-root-persist`) that adds passwordless sudo to the sudoers template.
- **Lateral movement:** logged in with stolen administrator credentials, raised Jump Item and Team roles to Administrator, then opened remote support sessions to internal endpoints with full file access. Took documents such as software inventories.
- **Anti-forensics:** deleted auth, SSL, HTTP and system logs.
- **Infrastructure:** commercial VPN exit nodes and compromised home routers. No public C2 IPs.
- **IOCs (SHA-256):**
  - SAWFLY `dc14e215269a93fdbc4c1a80b984c23faf7af0911c6dbb85309796a6f2ae95fb`
  - BARECLAW `1f19b9a27d79c11bda00a1e8faf74a7fdd683d38ccef1b60df2fd0307e3aa462`
  - Host artefacts: `/usr/bin/dbus-authd`, `zz-root-persist`, `NOPASSWD` in `sudoers.template`, the haproxy ACL `wswsws`, path `/uidchanarge`, port 19800.
- **Detection:**
  - GTIG YARA rules `G_Hunting_Webshell_SAWFLY_1` and `G_Hunting_Backdoor_BARECLAW_1`.
  - In BeyondTrust logs: `user_changed` events raising roles to Administrator, and unticketed `user_added`, `api_account` or `security_provider` events.
  - `/login` successes from residential or VPN addresses.
  - Unknown `.php` paths and WebSocket upgrades at the proxy.
  - Syslog gaps that line up with a single session.
- **Response:**
  - Apply BT26-05-RS. Versions older than 24.3.1 must upgrade first, and upgrading does not undo a compromise.
  - Treat any internet-reachable appliance that was vulnerable during the window as compromised: preserve evidence, rebuild and reissue its certificate.
  - Rotate credentials in dependency order, including **every credential typed into a support session during the window**.
  - Enforce SSO with phishing-resistant MFA, and keep privileged credentials out of the Remote Support vault.
- **Relevance:** remote-support tools hold the keys to the machines they support. Ask IT, managed service providers, outsourcers and law firms whether they run a self-hosted BeyondTrust Remote Support or PRA. Google warns that opportunistic mass exploitation may follow.
