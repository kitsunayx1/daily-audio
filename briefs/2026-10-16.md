## 1. Top three things to know

1. **Contractors are the breach.**
   - A security incident at **BAE Systems** stopped the Bankruptcy Noticing Center, which sends notices for every US federal bankruptcy court.
   - An attack on IT contractor **Senture** took down the US DOT's trucking safety contact centre.
   - A breach notice for **National Insurance Company** was issued by its law firm, Squire Patton Boggs, after that firm's own breach.
   - **Action:** restructuring and insolvency teams should expect delayed creditor notices and watch for notice-themed phishing. Ask key suppliers who runs their single points of failure.
2. **More appliances are being exploited, and CISA deadlines fall today and tomorrow.**
   - Ivanti Connect Secure's CISA deadline is **today (16 Oct)**, and a public detection script for the bug appeared on 15 Oct.
   - The PAN-OS and Cisco Secure Email Gateway deadlines are **17 Oct**.
   - **AhsayCBS**, a backup console widely used by managed service providers, is exploited and now in KEV.
   - **Langflow**, an AI workflow tool, is under attack on honeypots.
   - **Action:** ask managed service and backup providers whether they run AhsayCBS.
3. **Zero-click spyware delivered through online ads.**
   - Citizen Lab and Confiant say Israeli firm **Discover-Tech** used targeted ads to deliver Candiru spyware with no click needed.
   - At least 45 Al Jazeera devices were targeted. Ads carrying the script rendered on networks including NATO, the UN, EU bodies and the US DoD; that does not mean anyone there was infected.
   - **Action:** ad-blocking or filtering DNS on executive and travel devices directly reduces this risk.

## 2. Breaches and incidents

### BAE Systems incident halts the US federal Bankruptcy Noticing Center
- On 15 Oct the Administrative Office of the US Courts said an incident at contractor BAE Systems stopped production and distribution of bankruptcy notices for all federal bankruptcy courts.
- Down: bnc.uscourts.gov, bankruptcynotices.uscourts.gov, notices.uscourts.gov and the National Creditor Registration Service. PACER and CM/ECF are not reported as affected.
- BAE says the incident is "isolated to the BNC system". There is no restoration timeline, no named actor and no statement on data theft.
- **Why it matters:** one contractor runs a whole judicial process. Restructuring and creditor-advisory work, and clients in insolvency, will see delays. Expect phishing themed around bankruptcy notices.
- https://www.uscourts.gov/data-news/judiciary-news/2026/10/15/cybersecurity-incident-disrupting-bankruptcy-court-operations

### AhsayCBS backup console: CVE-2026-105133 and CVE-2026-105134 (exploited, KEV)
- An authentication bypass chained with unauthenticated RCE gives SYSTEM. Attackers dropped a JSP webshell and the XMRig miner. Huntress first saw exploitation on 7 Oct, with five organisations hit by 8 Oct.
- **Earlier fixes were incomplete.** Use hotfix 10.3.4.45 (v10) or 9.15.2.70 (v7–v9), reboot, restrict the management interface, and re-image hosts with IOCs because hidden backdoors were seen.
- **IOCs (Huntress):**
  - IPs: 177.4.12[.]11, 38.60.252[.]110, 107.191.47[.]199, 185.220.236[.]49, 104.234.26[.]10, 123.202.208[.]37.
  - Payload host: imagefiles-backup.oss-ap-southeast-7.aliyuncs[.]com. Service name `MicrosoftEdgeUpdateSvc`. Mining pool xmr.kryptex[.]network:8029.
  - SHA-256: msedge.exe `05f69ae6b2b89c1c4dcf836bff032232f11bf0109f2b498e2345045d06139034`; edge.exe `4dcb0202fe8b2d4d7b183764e38184cd6ed50132786cc7e7d1f7f4bce1dd6f3d`; Taskgmr.ps1 `481728a7c9c4c02be07051d9c1958d902ea6397ebb8952ab83944818e3d25d21`.
- **Detection:** unexpected child processes of `cbssvcX64.exe` or `cbssvcX86.exe` (Huntress Sigma rules).
- **Why it matters:** managed service providers and resellers run it, so it is a supplier risk.
- https://www.huntress.com/blog/ahsaycbs-flaws-exploit
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/ahsaycbs-flaws-exploited-to-deploy-web.html

### Langflow CVE-2026-105740 (CVSS 9.9, authenticated RCE, attacks on honeypots)
- Any logged-in user can add an MCP server whose command runs in bash, and it re-runs every time the server list loads.
- Affects versions before 1.9.0. Previdian logged 1,706 attempts from 28 IPs since 19 Sep, 316 of them on 15 Oct. Attackers try common passwords, then dump secrets.
- IOCs: 192.255.229[.]92; exfiltration to 146.70.211[.]112:18764.
- **Fix:** upgrade, set `LANGFLOW_AUTO_LOGIN=false`, and keep it off the internet. Not in KEV.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/langflows-critical-authenticated-rce.html

## 12. Threat actor deep dive: CL-UNK-1211, AI-assisted espionage against government Linux servers (Unit 42, 15 Oct)

- **Source:** https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/ai-campaign-targets-linux-infrastructure/
- **Who:** unattributed; Unit 42 has not decided between nation-state and criminal. Active October 2025 to April 2026.
- **Victims:** government bodies and state-linked infrastructure (countries not named), focusing on Linux web, Plesk/qmail mail and database servers.
- **Goal:** mailbox theft, and taking over social media accounts by finding recovery emails in government mailboxes.
- **Signs of AI help:** scripts with chatty step-by-step comments addressed to the operator, and rapid switching between PHP, JavaScript, Bash and Python. Suggests LLM-written code or an agent; the model is not identified.
- **Initial access:** PHP webshells, credential harvesting, and a custom Go scanner for React2Shell (CVE-2025-55182), not confirmed as the way in.
- **Tooling:** SysGoRAT (custom Go RAT over WebSocket, default 104.194.158[.]65:443), LinPEAS, PwnKit (CVE-2021-4034), revsocks, pysoxy and GSocket (disguised as `kcached` and `gs-dbus`). SUID backdoors such as `.libsystemd-helper`.
- **Persistence:** `auto_prepend_file` in every PHP config pointing to `/usr/lib/php/plesk_sys_driver.php`; `sys-update.service`; a five-minute cron job; SSH keys; on Windows a Run key and a scheduled task named "Onedrive".
- **Credential theft:** a `.bashrc` sudo alias logging passwords to `Solarwinds.StatusLog.txt` (a disguise; SolarWinds is not involved), modified login pages, config and history searches, and /etc/shadow.
- **Lateral movement:** MSSQL `xp_cmdshell`, including SQL-server-to-SQL-server hops, and SSH with `sshpass`.
- **Exfiltration:** mail and database dumps sent with nc, and uploads attempted to transfer.sh, bashupload.com, filebin.net and catbox.moe.
- **Covering tracks:** tried and failed to kill Cortex XDR; wiped logs twice; timestomping; and planted "RedTeamOps2025" and "pentest123" so it would look like an authorised test.
- **IOCs:**
  - IPs: 45.88.188[.]140, 198.23.185[.]234, 138.68.244[.]146, 185.21.136[.]6, 72.61.89[.]179, 104.194.158[.]65, 109.224.53[.]140, 162.243.175[.]162, 154.93.37[.]228, 141.164.58[.]87, 88.80.150[.]232, 88.119.167[.]57, 173.212.247[.]23, 104.248.131[.]53, 147.182.224[.]216, 93.93.112[.]57, 192.210.183[.]17.
  - Domains: magento[.]ink, solidmonpe[.]com, wibu-bau-bawang[.]online, beraskencur[.]site.
  - SHA-256 (selected): GSocket `d94f75a70b5cabaf786ac57177ed841732e62bdcc9a29e06e5b41d9be567bcfa`; backdoor `c527b94e3d21824a674face4cd9d862e536b60ccbf54573839e3360f99188343`; PwnKit `44e83f84a5d5219e2f7c3cf1e4f02489cae81361227f46946abe4b8d8245b879`; webshells `1505a3a727f8899b2ad343519f7bf9c438b8b802f8d6b854f5db3b52f857c76a`, `76c58fad4f70cf277a15a499b9a2151ac60e7a6b5c7a9ab684843dcc19f0d0a5`, `b1e82c6b0caea177e8bb71ad826867f509c65f0a43a651b50253e7c726566eac`. The full list is in the report.
- **Hunting:** php-fpm child processes; `mkfifo` with nc; `auto_prepend_file` changes; sudo aliases in `.bashrc`; unexpected authorized_keys; hidden binaries in /dev/shm and /tmp; pkill aimed at EDR; mass log truncation; outbound traffic to gsocket[.]io and file-sharing sites; `xp_cmdshell` being enabled.
- **Response:** rebuild affected hosts, rotate every credential found on them, patch CVE-2025-55182 and CVE-2021-4034, filter outbound traffic, and treat "red team" markers as possible deception.
- **Relevance:** the same techniques work against internet-facing Linux, Plesk and client-facing web and mail servers, and it is concrete evidence of AI-assisted intrusions.
