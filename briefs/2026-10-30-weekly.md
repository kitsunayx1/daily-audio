## 1. The week in brief

1. **Law, accounting and advisory firms were the target set, and a Big Four firm confirmed a vendor incident.**
   - Everest listed **PwC** (590 GB claimed from HR and learning systems); PwC confirmed an incident at an unnamed vendor hosting employee learning records and says no client data was involved. The countdown ended with no publication found by 29 Oct.
   - Leak sites named **Kirkland & Ellis**, **White & Case**, **Weitz & Luxenberg**, **Beasley Allen**, **Sanders Law Group**, **Morrison Foerster**, **Lowenstein Sandler** and **Morrison Cohen**, plus **Cherry Bekaert** (accounting and advisory) and **SK Capital Partners** (private equity). A plaintiff firm opened a class-action investigation into White & Case a day after an unverified listing.
   - CrowdStrike's legal report (28 Oct) counts 455 legal entities on leak sites (+39%) and CHATTY SPIDER (Silent Ransom Group) activity up 129%; Google and GuidePoint tie Falcon to UNC6671 / BlackFile help-desk vishing aimed at M&A and litigation data.
2. **PHP2Shell went from mass probing to KEV and firewalls, with a retraction on the way.**
   - Monday's lead, Unit 42's "first confirmed compromise" of a European government entity, was retracted on 27 Oct: the PHP2Shell link is unconfirmed and the entry point is unknown.
   - CISA added both CVEs to KEV on 26 Oct (due 29 Oct); honeypots recorded 35 working shells on 24 Oct; on 28 Oct Palo Alto confirmed exploitation of internet-exposed PAN-OS management interfaces, and CISA added Linux "Fragnesia", which is chained with PHP2Shell for root (due 31 Oct).
   - The Brotli flaw ("Node2Shell") also entered KEV (due 10 Nov), with VMware SDDC Manager still unpatched.
3. **Vendors and SaaS platforms leaked their customers' data, at scale.**
   - Yumiru Link's **Cuenote FC** email service: 51.6M records across 49 client systems, then Saison Card phishing sent from its own domain. **blastmail** reached 344 contracts and 119+ downstream notices.
   - **Railway**, **Amplitude** (via Coinbase), **Amadeus** (JAL, Korean Air, Asiana), **Okta sign-on** (40,000+ DHS staff records), **Genesys**, **Frontline Education**, **RMS Cloud**, **ENGIE**'s marketing partner and **Warp**'s vendor all exposed data held for others.
   - Test and staging environments kept appearing as the leak point: PwC's suggested staging tenant (unconfirmed), University of Copenhagen, KOMOJU.
4. **The help desk and the session token were the front door.**
   - Arctic Wolf counted 400+ Microsoft Teams "IT support" intrusions (STAC4749); Huntress showed post-MFA tokens replayed at scale ("token spraying") and victims enrolled into attacker-owned MDM.
   - Silent Ransom Group hired a man to pose as IT staff in person at a US law firm (CrowdStrike); Falcon phones staff as fake IT help desks.
   - The FEC took e-filing offline for about 41 hours after "a bad actor attempting to breach FEC systems", six days before the US midterms.
5. **AI agents attacked, and developer pipelines were drained.**
   - South Korean police say one suspect used generative AI to breach about 90 organisations; Cathedral's agents reached Anthropic's sales, HR and IT data in under 12 hours (authorised test); TrendAI described 44-agent CyberStrikeAI intrusions in Japan; 123 Reg called its breach "AI-driven"; AI tooling was found on PHP2Shell attacker infrastructure.
   - **Gheist** used stolen GitHub tokens on 850+ accounts to dump CI/CD secrets; LiteLLM's root cause traced back to a master key leaked in May; the testmetest worm and a Downloads-folder AGENTS.md planted instructions for AI coding assistants.
   - OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Terra meets its "Critical" cyber threshold and released it generally at half the price of its predecessor.

**Elections, in one line:** Serbia and Brazil voted on 25 Oct and Israel on 27 Oct with no reported election-day cyber disruption; the FEC's filing outage is the one concrete incident before the US midterms on 3 Nov.

### Brotli / Node2Shell (CVE-2026-111497) [UPDATE, 23–29 Oct]

- **Wider blast radius (26 Oct):** the bug is in Google's Brotli library 1.0.3 to 1.2.0. Fixed: libbrotli 1.0.10 / 1.1.1 / 1.2.1; Bun 1.4.4; .NET 8.0.33 / 9.0.22 / 10.0.14 (out-of-band, 25 Oct); Envoy 1.40.0 / 1.39.3 / 1.38.6 / 1.37.8; Istio 1.31.2 / 1.30.6 / 1.29.9; Python Brotli 1.2.1; brotlicffi 1.2.1.0; Ruby brotli 0.9.1. Updating the system library does not fix bundled copies.
- **Latest:** KEV 27 Oct, due 10 Nov. **VMware SDDC Manager** affected (VMSA-2026-0008, 9.1.1 rated 9.8); VMSA-2026-0008.1 (28 Oct) still "patch pending" for every line; workarounds KB460732 and KB460734.
