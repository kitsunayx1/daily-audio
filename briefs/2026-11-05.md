## 1. Top three

1. **Russia's SVR is reported to be hunting phones through trusted websites.** Sekoia attributes, with "moderate to high confidence", a watering-hole campaign to APT29. It used compromised sites (including a major US chemical-industry trade association), lookalikes and a hijacked analytics script to push patched iOS and Android exploit chains. 2. **Third parties keep paying out.** Crédit Mutuel Arkéa confirmed a theft of about €118M / US$133M through a compromised service provider. Akira and Brain Cipher listed CPA firms. Silent Ransom Group added two more redacted entries. A new group, WhiteArmor, claims Unilever (section 3).

## 12. Deep dive: APT29 "Poisoned Source", watering holes against iPhones and Android (Sekoia, 4 Nov)

**Why it matters:** Russian SVR espionage aimed at the phones of people who visit trade-association, political, defence and hospitality websites. One compromised site is a major US chemical-industry trade association, exactly the kind of site consultants and clients visit. The iOS chain empties Mail, iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, Keychain and iCloud Drive. Every exploit is already patched, so the exposure is old or unpatched phones.
