Action: brief reception and the service desk on verifying anyone who calls or visits as "IT," and enforce USB controls.

- **Entry vector:** not established. Claroty previously disclosed authentication bypass and RCE flaws (fixed in R4.3.1), and about 1,590 exposed systems are in France.

## 11. Threat actor deep dive: ShinyHunters (UNC6240)

- **What it does:** SaaS and web-application data theft followed by extortion. The FBI says the group and its associates breached more than 140 organisations and collected over $70M in the past year.
- **This week:** the third arrest in weeks. Edward Dubrovsky, a Toronto ransomware negotiator, was arrested in Pennsylvania and charged with extortion conspiracy and Hobbs Act extortion; the complaint is sealed. Earlier arrests were in the Netherlands (15 Sept) and Jordan (28 Sept, now cooperating). The group's Tor site is offline. Krebs reports that executives at other negotiation firms may face charges.
- **TTPs (Mandiant, 25 Sept):**
  - WAF bypass by URL-encoding the path (`/%50SEMHUB/`), then Java deserialization in the PeopleSoft PSEMHUB servlet.
  - Web shells x.jsp and u.jsp, Neo-reGeorg tunnels and MeshCentral.
  - The SIDEEYE backdoor, delivered in a trojanized Light Alloy installer signed with an EV certificate.
- **IOCs:**
  - IPs: 5.199.162.157, 104.219.234.138 and 162.219.30.165 (ports 3333/3334)
  - Domain: winmanage-me[.]network
  - SHA-256 hashes:
    - x.jsp `48b4a0827da7bbfce9fb52464f8a659dea7a035189c52c506c0bfb4b1c3fe494`

    - Ple64.exe `3ba215692665513abfffd4e815c5c45f2d41e5dcc4283a2a3b740930c5c417c3`
- **Why it matters:**
