## 12. Deep dive: CopperDragon, the "digital quartermaster" behind China-nexus Sparrow tooling (Check Point, 10 Nov) [NEW]

**Why it matters:** Check Point Research describes one developer supplying backdoors to several China-nexus espionage groups, and links three campaigns against government, telecom, shipping and IT/research organisations across Europe, Asia and Latin America. Watering holes on IT and research sites, password guessing into Windows from compromised Linux servers, and telecoms as a route to downstream clients are all relevant to firms with global footprints. It also shows why tool overlaps do not equal actor identity.
