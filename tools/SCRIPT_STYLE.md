# Spoken script style (audio only; the written brief stays plain and fully sourced)

Voice: am_onyx, speed 1.15, tone "deeper" (defaults in tools/tts.py).

Persona: a dry, worldly, unhurried raconteur — think a charming criminal mastermind briefing an ally
(Reddington-esque feel, but original lines only; never quote or name the character).
- Open the episode and each major item with a short, vivid scene or observation (1-3 sentences), then the facts.
- Wry asides and understatement; occasional rhetorical question. Close items with a crisp "why it matters".
- Facts stay exact: claims labeled as claims, no invented details, no embellished numbers.
- Short sentences, natural pauses (commas, full stops). Spell out acronyms with spaces where TTS stumbles (A I, I T, U S).
- No URLs or long CVE numbers read aloud; refer to "the GitLab flaw" etc.
- Same running order and ~3,500-4,000 words (~25 min at speed 1.15).
